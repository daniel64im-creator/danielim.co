Real-Time Demo Booking Alerts

Gave leadership real-time visibility into qualifying demo bookings by replacing manual CRM monitoring with an event-triggered Salesforce Flow that routed filtered alerts to the right stakeholders.

Problem
Manual CRM monitoring for bookings No automated booking alerts Leadership visibility gaps Risk of unfiltered notification noise
Solution
Event-based trigger logic Qualification filters (valid demos only) Targeted stakeholder routing Duplicate alert prevention
Outcome
Real-time booking visibility No manual monitoring needed High-quality alert signals only Improved leadership transparency
How It Works
Demo Booked → Qualification Logic Evaluated → Notification Conditions Met → Targeted Stakeholder Alert Sent
